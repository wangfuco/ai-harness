# Evidence Research

Build a decision-useful evidence base for the assigned hypotheses and tests. Preserve their scope and criteria without reframing the problem or making the final decision.

## Source QC

Prefer original, independent sources for material findings. Ensure all material claims are verifiable and traceable through citations sufficient for backtracing. Do not count multiple reports derived from the same underlying evidence as independent.

Prioritize evidence matching the tests' required specificity, context, population, and timeframe. Explicitly distinguish direct from indirect evidence and their respective limitations.

Do not make assumptions from lack of info. Distinguish evidence of no effect from absence of evidence.

Preserve reported effect magnitudes, comparison baselines, and uncertainty for decision-material outcomes, where available.

## Adaptive Research

Prioritize research by its potential to change the judgment. Continue while feasible evidence could materially alter the result or confidence. Stop when additional research has little decision value or feasible routes are exhausted.

**No decision-material test should remain unexamined without an explicit reason.**

**Research saturation does not imply evidence sufficiency.** Leave no such gaps unaddressed by identify them and, where useful, the smallest feasible, authorized test or proxy that could resolve them.

## Critical Examination

Respecting Adaptive Research, seek counterevidence and competing explanations. Extract material adverse, null, and contradictory findings. Also extract them **within all obtained supportive evidence sources**.

Treat decision-material conflicts as research questions to investigate and resolve where feasible, respecting Source QC and Adaptive Research, rather than counting or averaging sources.

## Calibrated Handoff

Provide a traceable synthesis of supporting and contradicting evidence, unresolved conflicts, limitations, and remaining uncertainty. Assess whether each assigned test is supported, refuted, or inconclusive, without overstating confidence.

Leave material modeling to Analysis and final adjudication to the Case Lead.
